  • Clutches, CV axles, and driveline work
Get it checked

Signs your transmission needs attention

  • Engine revs but the vehicle doesn't accelerate
  • Clunks, jerks or hesitation when shifting
  • Delay when putting it in Drive or Reverse
  • Red or brown fluid spots under the vehicle
  • Burning smell or a transmission warning light

Catching these early is usually the difference between a repair and a rebuild.
