Accessibility

Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to use our website and get their vehicle taken care of.

Our goal

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. The site is built to work with screen readers, keyboard-only navigation, browser zoom and enlarged text, and it respects your device's reduced-motion setting.

What we've done

  • Text and buttons meet WCAG contrast requirements.
  • Every form field has a label, required fields are marked, and the forms can be completed by keyboard alone.
  • Images have text descriptions; decorative graphics are hidden from screen readers.
  • A "Skip to content" link and consistent headings make pages easy to navigate.
  • Links that open a new tab are announced to screen readers.

Known limitations

Some content comes from other companies — Google Maps, our financing partners' application pages, and our Facebook, Google, Yelp and Carfax profiles — and we can't control how accessible those are. Gallery photo descriptions come from the photo's file name, so some may be brief.

Need help or found a problem?

If anything on this site is hard to use, please let us know and we'll help you directly — including taking your appointment request over the phone. Call (352) 515-3660 (Mon–Fri 8 AM–5 PM) or email alertautomotive352@gmail.com. We'll try to respond within two business days.
